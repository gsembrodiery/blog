Our Story

Rooted in Tradition

"Every thread we stitch carries the soul of Garividi — our heritage, our pride, our craft."

Born in the heart of Garividi, Vizianagaram District, GS Computer Embroidery is a family-run studio where traditional craftsmanship meets modern embroidery technology. We've been adorning families across Andhra Pradesh with our handcrafted and machine-embroidered garments.

From delicate floral machine embroideries on kurtis to bold custom motifs on contemporary garments, every piece from our studio is stitched with love, precision, and a deep respect for textile artistry.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Where is GS Computer Embroidery located?

We are on Main Road, Garividi, Vizianagaram District, Andhra Pradesh – 535 101. Find us on the map below.

Can I bring my own design for embroidery?

Yes. Share a sketch, photo or reference and we will digitize it and stitch it with machine embroidery on your fabric.

Do you accept bulk orders such as school uniform logos and festival costumes?

Yes, bulk and logo embroidery orders are welcome. Message us on WhatsApp with the quantity and design for a quote.

How long does an embroidery or stitching order take?

Timing depends on design complexity and order size. Tell us your occasion date on WhatsApp and we will confirm a delivery time.

Do you do both embroidery and tailoring?

Yes. We handle embroidery, blouse and dress stitching, and complete garments under one roof.
